# FV-04 Rubric

| Area | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Legal random generation | 25 | Required transaction count is generated and every selector is validated |
| Constraint design | 25 | Reusable legal constraint and purposeful inline overflow constraint work as intended |
| Failure handling | 20 | Every randomize call is checked and the contradictory case fails for the expected reason |
| Reproducibility and diagnostics | 20 | Seed behavior is demonstrated and generated transactions are identifiable |
| Reflection | 10 | Learner explains constraints, seeds, failure, and directed/random roles |

Passing requires at least 75 points, a passing normal run, and evidence from repeated and changed seeds.
