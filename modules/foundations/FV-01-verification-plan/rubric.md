# FV-01 Rubric

| Area | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Requirement traceability | 30 | Every specification requirement maps to purposeful test cases |
| Boundary and negative-case intent | 25 | Arithmetic boundaries, flag transitions, and invalid selectors are meaningfully exercised |
| Observation, prediction, and failure criteria | 25 | Expected outputs are independent, observable, and unambiguous |
| Feasibility and clarity | 10 | Cases are implementable and avoid redundant or vague intent |
| Explanation and reflection | 10 | Reflection connects the plan to future self-checking implementation |

Passing requires at least 75 points and no missing specification requirement. The structural checker is necessary but not sufficient; semantic review determines the score.
