# FV-02 Rubric

| Area | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Functional checking and fault detection | 35 | Every planned case checks all four outputs; an incorrect expectation fails |
| Separation and reuse | 20 | A reusable task separates case data from drive, sample, compare, and report mechanics |
| Combinational sampling | 15 | Outputs are sampled after an explicit nonzero settling delay |
| Test completion and diagnostics | 20 | Checks and errors are counted; pass/fail and case-level mismatches are unambiguous |
| Reflection | 10 | Learner explains prediction, observation, checking, and proof of fault detection |

Passing requires at least 75 points, a passing normal run, and a demonstrated negative/fault run.
