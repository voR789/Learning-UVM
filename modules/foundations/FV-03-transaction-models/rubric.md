# FV-03 Rubric

| Area | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Transaction representation | 20 | Related stimulus and expected fields are grouped coherently |
| Handle and alias reasoning | 25 | Same-object alias behavior is predicted, exercised, and explained |
| Independent copy and compare | 30 | Every field is copied; equality and deliberate mismatch are detected |
| Diagnostics and completion | 15 | Useful transaction strings and an unambiguous pass/fail result are produced |
| Reflection | 10 | Learner connects object semantics to future verification components |

Passing requires at least 75 points and executable evidence that compare detects a changed field.
