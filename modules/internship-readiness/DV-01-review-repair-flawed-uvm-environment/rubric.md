# DV-01 rubric

Passing requires at least 80/100 and no critical correctness failure.

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Reproducible initial evidence and identity/state observations | 20 |
| Correct root-cause analysis at the first violated boundary | 25 |
| Smallest correct repair preserving stable published observations | 25 |
| Repaired pass plus direct fault detection | 15 |
| Concise debug plan and evidence summary | 10 |
| Reflection and reusable ownership rule | 5 |

Critical failures: weakening/bypassing the scoreboard, changing expected results to match the defect, hiding the failure with timing, or failing to detect the direct fault.
