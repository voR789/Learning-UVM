# DV-03 rubric

Passing requires at least 75/100 and no critical coverage-integrity failure.

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Correct reachable-versus-unreachable classification | 25 |
| Minimal targeted legal stimulus closes all required holes | 30 |
| Passive observed proof and baseline-fault detection | 25 |
| Specification-backed disposition evidence | 15 |
| Concise coverage-integrity explanation | 5 |

Critical failures: excluding a reachable required bin, directly setting coverage state, stimulating prohibited behavior to inflate coverage, weakening the passive grader, or claiming closure without executable observation.
