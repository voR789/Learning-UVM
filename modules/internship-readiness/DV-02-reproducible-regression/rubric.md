# DV-02 rubric

Passing requires at least 75/100 and no critical reproducibility or triage failure.

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Matrix design: health anchor, randomized diversity, and stated intent | 15 |
| Complete execution despite failures; every outcome retained | 20 |
| Exact reproduction of each distinct failure signature | 20 |
| Root-cause-oriented bucketing supported by cross-run evidence | 30 |
| Falsifiable next experiment and concise engineering communication | 15 |

Critical failures: stopping at the first red run, losing test/seed identity, classifying only by final report text, hiding failures to produce a green regression, or claiming a root cause without reproducible evidence.
