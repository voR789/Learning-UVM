# UB-02 Rubric

| Area | Points |
|---|---:|
| Correct factory registration and construction | 25 |
| Correct build/run phase responsibilities | 20 |
| Exactly three time-separated worker ticks | 15 |
| Deliberate objection and completion handling | 20 |
| Live-state checking and useful diagnostics | 10 |
| Explanation | 10 |

Passing requires at least 75 points, the documented run to pass, and no critical
failure in construction, checking, or termination.
