# Future practice backlog

## From-scratch UVM module repetitions

After completing the current curriculum, practice building verification modules
from scratch rather than from supplied scaffolding. Repeat the exercise format
until the UVM structure and responsibility boundaries are understood without
reference material.

Focus on independently choosing and connecting the test, environment, agent,
sequencer, driver, monitor, scoreboard, coverage subscriber, and termination
mechanism for a small unfamiliar DUT.
